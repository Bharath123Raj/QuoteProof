01 · RETRIEVE

Traceable market retrieval

SerpApi Google Shopping supplies structured listing prices and seller names. A matching aggregate card can be expanded with SerpApi Immersive Product to obtain individual store offers. Otherwise a targeted Shopping query is allowed. Google Search finds specification and warranty pages. Every retrieval keeps its ID, engine, query and timestamp.

02 · CHALLENGE

Reject misleading comparisons

All required identifiers must appear in the title. Model suffix conflicts, refurbished goods, accessories and spare parts (including formatter boards and replacement scanner units), bundles (including mouse/webcam combinations), unrequested Mac or MX Mini Business editions, or AZERTY/QWERTZ/DVORAK layouts, starting/range prices, installment amounts, missing links, unidentified sellers and non-INR prices are excluded. Desidime deal posts are discovery only and do not count as seller offers. Matching is a transparent heuristic, not manufacturer verification.

03 · DECIDE

Use a robust benchmark

The benchmark is the median of the lowest eligible listing from each distinct named seller. At least three sellers are required. A price spread above 60% of the median pauses the recommendation. A quote more than 10% above the benchmark is flagged for negotiation.

What this report establishes

A reproducible comparison of discovered listing prices. Checkout costs preserve reported tax, shipping and total fields when present, and flag missing or inconsistent components. They describe one listing checkout and do not establish bulk landed cost, seller legitimacy, warranty eligibility, live inventory or bulk supply. Web snippets are shown for follow-up, never treated as verified claims. No language model invents prices or approves a purchase.

Requirements evidence

A separate Shopping query discovers up to six candidates for the entered category, quantity, base-price budget and up to six feature phrases. At most three product-detail tokens are expanded. Up to three unresolved candidates receive a model-specific Google search on known manufacturer domains (HP, Brother, Samsung, Logitech). Only matching model titles supply snippet evidence; other links remain discovery leads. Only matching detail titles supply product specifications. Feature text is marked as mentioned, conflicting or unknown, never independently verified. Numeric thresholds need manual checking. Choosing a candidate creates a separate quote draft; it never changes another model’s benchmark.

Search budget and privacy

Maximum 3 lines and 12 outbound requests per audit when cost enrichment is enabled (9 otherwise). Requirements discovery is separate: one Shopping search, up to three product-details lookups and up to three Google specification searches (7 maximum). Each line uses Shopping and Google discovery. When fewer than three matching sellers are found, one product-details lookup or targeted Shopping follow-up is allowed, never both. Cost enrichment can add one product-details lookup if one has not already run and a matching token exists. Search origin: New Delhi, India; domain: google.co.in. Successful searches are cached in server memory for 30 minutes, isolated by a key fingerprint. The cache is temporary and may disappear between server instances. Fresh search bypasses this cache; SerpApi can still return its own cached results. Shared searches require sign-in on Sites and use a server secret, with persistent monthly, per-user daily and minute request limits. The loopback local server uses owner access. Limits count upstream attempts, including failures, rather than billed credits. Personal searches use only the key you enter. The entered key is kept only in page memory and sent to this app’s server, then SerpApi. Watchlists retain up to 30 checks per product in this browser. Watch rechecks use one Shopping search per model (at most three per batch), with optional upstream cache bypass. Target alerts appear in the watchlist when a usable matching-listing median reaches the target. Repeated search IDs and cached checks do not add fresh observations. Quote inputs, requirement drafts, watchlists and reports are stored in this browser and can be cleared below. Refresh restores your latest quote; a new audit is required to update results.
